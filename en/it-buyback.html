Turn end-of-life IT into revenue

Instead of paying for disposal, Tech Recycling Berlin pays you a market-fair price for your high-value IT hardware. We assess each device individually, document its condition, and pay fairly — after GDPR-compliant data erasure.

This is modern IT asset buyback: sustainable, economical, and fully documented.

What we buy

  • Laptops, notebooks, ultrabooks (business-grade)
  • Workstations and desktop PCs
  • Rack and tower servers, storage systems
  • Networking equipment (switches, routers, firewalls)
  • Monitors, thin clients, docking stations
  • Tablets, smartphones, and mobile devices
