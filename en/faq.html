Frequently asked

Answers to your questions

Everything you need to know about IT disposal, data erasure, and our service — at a glance.

Is your data erasure GDPR-compliant?

Yes. We erase data carriers with Blancco according to BSI-GS or BSI-GSK. For every erasure you receive an erasure certificate showing device, serial number, method and time. Data carriers that cannot be erased are physically destroyed according to DIN 66399.

Do you pick up hardware in Berlin?

Yes, we offer free pickup in Berlin and the surrounding area. From individual devices to entire server rooms — we handle logistics, transport, and packaging.

Which devices do you accept?

We take all IT hardware: laptops, PCs, servers, networking equipment, monitors, printers, smartphones, tablets, hard drives, SSDs, storage media, and related peripherals. We do not accept loose batteries or rechargeable batteries; batteries built into a device are no problem. We work exclusively for businesses, public authorities and institutions, not for private individuals.

Do I receive payment for my old IT?

For high-value hardware, we provide a market-fair purchase offer after data erasure. That turns your end-of-life IT into revenue rather than a disposal cost. We buy Apple devices up to five years and Windows devices up to three years from the date of purchase; we do not buy monitors or network equipment.

How quickly will I receive a quote?

You typically receive a bespoke, non-binding quote within 24 hours — by phone, email, or via our contact form.

Do you offer on-site service in Berlin?

Yes. On request we perform data erasure and hard-drive destruction directly on your premises — your data never leaves the building. Ideal for highly sensitive environments such as law firms, banks, or public authorities.

Which certificates will I receive?

Depending on the service: erasure certificate for every erasure, disposal certificate under WEEE/ElektroG – data carriers physically destroyed per DIN 66399 are listed on it separately – and the pickup receipt you get at handover. All audit-proof.

How much does IT disposal cost?

Costs depend on type, quantity, and condition of the hardware. For high-value devices, we often pay you rather than charge. Every quote is individual — we respond within 24 hours.

Is your data erasure BSI-compliant?

Yes. Software-based erasure is carried out with certified erasure software according to BSI-GS or BSI-GSK (German Federal Office for Information Security). DIN 66399, by contrast, applies only to physical destruction — for data carriers that cannot be erased.

Do you serve clients outside Berlin?

Yes. We're headquartered in Berlin but serve clients across Germany. For larger projects, we come to you nationwide — with sealed transport and full documentation.

What happens to recycled devices?

High-value devices are reused or resold after certified data erasure. Hardware that can't be reused is professionally dismantled and the raw materials returned to the cycle. Over 98% is recovered.

Is pickup insured?

Our transport is fully insured. On request, we offer sealed transport with GPS tracking — important for banks, insurers, and public authorities.

Didn't find your question?

Call us — we're happy to advise you personally and at no obligation.
