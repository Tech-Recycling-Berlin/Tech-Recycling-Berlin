When erasure isn't enough — mechanical destruction

For defective data carriers or the highest security requirements, physical destruction is the only safe way. We shred hard drives and SSDs such that data recovery is technically impossible.

Our processes fulfil DIN 66399 protection classes up to H5 — the highest standard for highly sensitive data, as used by public authorities, banks, or healthcare.

Our destruction methods

  • Shredding — mechanical reduction to particles up to protection class H5
  • Degaussing — demagnetisation of magnetic data carriers
  • Drilling — mobile on-site destruction with heavy-duty drill
  • High-security shredder for SSDs, smartphones, and special media
DIN 66399 classes

H1 to H5 available

We select the appropriate method based on your data-protection requirements. H5 = max particle size 10 mm².
