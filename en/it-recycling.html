Responsible IT recycling for businesses

E-waste contains both valuable raw materials such as copper, gold, palladium, and rare earths, as well as hazardous substances like mercury or brominated flame retardants. Proper recycling is therefore not just legally required — it is active environmental protection.

Tech Recycling Berlin is a certified waste-management operator under EfbV. We separate, sort, and recycle your end-of-life IT to the highest standards and issue you a legally valid disposal certificate.

What we recycle

  • Computers, laptops, tablets, smartphones
  • Monitors, printers, scanners, copiers
  • Servers, network equipment, UPS systems
  • Cables, chargers, peripherals
  • Small electronic devices and components

Our recycling process

From old device to raw material — in five steps

Intake

Quantity recording, waste shipping documents, and transport per waste classification.

Sorting

Pre-sorting by device class and material group at our Berlin intake facility.

Dismantling

Manual disassembly, hazardous-material removal, and separation of reusable components.

Recovery

Material separation into metals, plastics, and electronic fractions for further processing.
