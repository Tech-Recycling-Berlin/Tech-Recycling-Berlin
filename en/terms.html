Terms & Conditions

Valid for all services of Tech Recycling Berlin to business customers within the meaning of § 14 BGB.

§ 1 Scope

These Terms and Conditions apply to all contracts between Tech Recycling Berlin (hereinafter "Provider") and its clients regarding services in IT disposal, data erasure, IT buyback, IT recycling, hard-drive destruction, server disposal, and data recovery. Deviating terms of the client are not recognised unless the Provider expressly agrees to them in writing.

The Provider concludes contracts exclusively with businesses within the meaning of § 14 BGB, legal entities under public law, and special funds under public law. No contracts are concluded with consumers within the meaning of § 13 BGB; hardware is not accepted from private individuals.

§ 2 Conclusion of contract

Offers from the Provider are non-binding. A contract is concluded only through written order confirmation or commencement of performance.

§ 3 Scope of services

The specific scope of services is defined in the individual quote or order confirmation. The Provider performs its services according to the current state of the art and applicable legal regulations, in particular GDPR, BDSG, and WEEE/ElektroG.

Loose batteries and rechargeable batteries are not accepted, including replacement batteries from uninterruptible power supplies (UPS). Batteries built into a device are not affected by this.

§ 4 Prices and payment terms

Quotes are based on the client's information about the type, quantity, and condition of the hardware and are an estimate. Invoicing is based on what was actually handed over at collection and subsequently inspected and processed; the invoice is authoritative.

Prices are final prices. The Provider is a small business within the meaning of § 19 UStG (German VAT Act); therefore no VAT is charged or shown. Invoices are due without deduction within 14 days of the invoice date.

§ 5 IT buyback

Apple devices up to five years old and Windows devices up to three years old are bought, in each case counted from the date of purchase. Monitors and network equipment (switches, routers, firewalls, access points) are not bought but taken over for recycling.

Buyback prices are subject to inspection by the Provider. The client's statements about condition are not a basis for valuation; the condition determined during inspection is authoritative. Devices registered in a mobile device management system or in Apple Business Manager and not released cannot be fully inspected and are valued conservatively.

If the client declines the buyback offer after inspection, the Provider returns the devices free of charge or disposes of them as instructed by the client. Payment is made after receipt and inspection of the hardware.

§ 6 Client obligations

The client confirms that they are the owner of the handed-over hardware or have the necessary authority to dispose of it. They are obliged to create their own data backups before handover if desired.

§ 7 Data protection and erasure

The Provider undertakes to treat all personal data obtained during performance of services confidentially and to process it in accordance with statutory regulations.

Data carriers are erased with certified erasure software according to BSI-GS or BSI-GSK; as proof, the client receives the erasure certificate generated by the erasure software. Data carriers that cannot be erased by software, for example because they are defective or locked, are physically destroyed according to DIN 66399.

§ 8 Liability

The Provider is liable without limitation for intent and gross negligence, and for damages resulting from injury to life, body, or health. For slight negligence the Provider is liable only for breach of essential contractual obligations and limited to foreseeable, contract-typical damage.

§ 9 Jurisdiction and applicable law

Place of jurisdiction is Berlin. The law of the Federal Republic of Germany applies, excluding UN sales law.

§ 10 Severability

Should individual provisions of these Terms be invalid or unenforceable, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected.
