FAQ

Frequently asked questions about server disposal

Are the hard drives in servers erased?

Yes. A server with built-in hard drives is erased like any other data carrier, and you receive an erasure certificate for every erasure.

What happens to switches, routers and firewalls?

Network equipment is neither erased nor bought; it is taken over for recycling.

Do you need a device list in advance?

It helps, but it is not required. With a list or a few photos we can estimate in advance and plan the right vehicle. What counts is what is actually handed over and counted at pickup.

Which proof do I receive?

An erasure certificate for every erasure and a disposal certificate for the job, which lists physically destroyed data carriers separately.
