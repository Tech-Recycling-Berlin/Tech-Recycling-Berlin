Frequently asked

Key answers at a glance

Is your data erasure GDPR-compliant?

Yes. We erase all data carriers following BSI and DIN 66399 standards and issue a legally valid erasure certificate documenting the complete and irreversible destruction of your data.

Do you pick up hardware in Berlin?

Yes, we offer free pickup in Berlin and the surrounding area. From individual devices to entire server rooms — we handle logistics, transport, and packaging. Sealed transport and chain-of-custody available on request.

Which devices do you accept?

We take all IT hardware: laptops, PCs, servers, networking equipment, monitors, printers, smartphones, tablets, hard drives, SSDs, storage media, and related peripherals. Cables and smaller components too.

Do I receive payment for my old IT?

For high-value hardware, we provide a market-fair purchase offer after data erasure. That turns your end-of-life IT into revenue rather than a disposal cost — full valuation transparency included.

How quickly will I receive a quote?

You typically receive a bespoke, non-binding quote within 24 hours — by phone, email, or via our contact form. Same-day response available for time-critical projects.

Do you offer on-site service in Berlin?

Yes. On request we perform data erasure and hard-drive destruction directly on your premises — your data never leaves the building. Ideal for highly sensitive environments such as law firms, banks, or public authorities.
