About us

Tech Recycling Berlin — Your partner for sustainable IT disposal

For years we've handled IT disposal, data erasure, and recycling for businesses, authorities, and institutions in Berlin and across Germany.

Our mission: Data secure, raw materials in circulation.

We believe IT disposal has to be more than throwing things away. Every device contains valuable raw materials and often sensitive data — both deserve respect, care, and professional handling.

Tech Recycling Berlin combines both: maximum data security through certified processes and maximum resource recovery through professional recycling. Our goal: your old IT becomes a new device — not e-waste in a landfill on the other side of the world.

What sets us apart

  • Regionally rooted — headquartered in Berlin-Tiergarten, fast response times in the region
  • Certified waste-management operator under EfbV, ElektroG, and GDPR
  • Transparent processes — a documented certificate for every step
  • Personal points of contact, not a call centre
  • Fair quotes — market-appropriate valuation, no hidden fees
