Why professional data erasure is essential

When you decommission laptops, hard drives, SSDs, smartphones, or servers, sensitive data often remains recoverable long after deleting files or formatting. GDPR requires you to destroy personal data verifiably and irreversibly — violations carry substantial fines.

Tech Recycling Berlin provides you with legally compliant, documented data erasure that meets all regulatory requirements — from GDPR to industry standards such as ISO 27001 or TISAX.

Our erasure standards

  • BSI guidelines (German Federal Office for Information Security)
  • DIN 66399 — protection classes up to H5 (highest protection)
  • NIST SP 800-88 Clear / Purge / Destroy
  • Multi-pass overwriting to DoD 5220.22-M for magnetic media
  • Cryptographic Erase for SSDs and modern storage media

What our data erasure includes

Software erasure

Multi-pass overwriting of all sectors with certified erasure software. For functional HDDs, SSDs, and storage media — devices can subsequently be reused or sold.

On-site service in Berlin

We come to your location and carry out the erasure in front of you. Ideal for law firms, banks, authorities, and highly sensitive environments — your data never leaves your building.
