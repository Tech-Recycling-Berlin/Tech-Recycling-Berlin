Data Recovery — In a certified cleanroom lab

Professional recovery of lost data from failed hard drives, SSDs, RAID systems, and memory cards — together with our specialist partner in a certified cleanroom lab.

Free initial analysis Certified cleanroom HDD · SSD · RAID · NAS GDPR-compliant

When data seems lost — we bring it back

A failed drive, an accidental format, a RAID failure: data loss is a nightmare for any business. With professional data recovery in our partner's cleanroom lab, we can achieve full recovery in most cases.

What we can recover

  • Hard drives (HDD) — mechanical and logical damage
  • SSDs and NVMe drives — controller and flash failures
  • RAID systems (0, 1, 5, 6, 10) — rebuild and reconstruction
  • USB sticks, SD cards, CF cards
  • Server and NAS storage
  • Deleted files and formatted drives

Common causes

  • Head crash or mechanical damage (clicking sounds)
  • Water and fire damage
  • SSD controller failures
  • Accidental deletion or formatting
  • File-system corruption and malware
